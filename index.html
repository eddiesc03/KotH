Promotion & relegation

  1. The winner of each group is promoted to a higher group, except the topmost one — Group A's winner becomes King of the Hive for the season.
  2. The bottom two finishers of Groups A, B1 and B2 are relegated to lower groups.
  3. To keep players moving through the lower half of the pyramid, Groups C1–C4 and D1–D3 each send their top two up, and Groups C1–C4 also send their bottom two down. Group D is the bottom level, so nobody is relegated out of it.
  4. Next season, players are reshuffled between groups on the same level so nobody repeats the same opponents.
